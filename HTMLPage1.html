About his life

Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890) was a Dutch painter who became one of the most influential artists in the history of Western art. He was born on March 30, 1853, in Zundert, a small town in the Netherlands. Although he is now recognized around the world for his unique artistic style, he struggled to find success and recognition during his lifetime. Before becoming a full-time artist, Van Gogh worked in different fields, including art dealing, teaching, and religious work. In 1880, he decided to dedicate himself to painting. He began studying drawing and experimenting with different techniques, focusing on ordinary people, landscapes, and everyday life. His early works often used dark colors and showed the difficult living conditions of workers and peasants. In 1886, Van Gogh moved to Paris, where he met other artists and discovered new approaches to painting. During this period, he became interested in Impressionism and Japanese prints. These influences encouraged him to experiment with brighter colors, stronger contrasts, and more expressive brushstrokes. His style gradually became more personal and recognizable. In 1888, he moved to Arles, in southern France, hoping to find inspiration in the countryside and its sunlight. There, he created some of his most famous paintings, including Sunflowers and The Bedroom. He used intense yellows, blues, and greens to express emotions and capture the beauty of everyday scenes. His paintings became known for their movement, texture, and vivid colors. Van Gogh also painted many self-portraits, landscapes, flowers, and scenes of daily life. One of his most famous works, The Starry Night, was created in 1889 while he was staying at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence. The painting shows a swirling night sky above a small village and reflects his distinctive use of color, movement, and imagination. Throughout his life, Van Gogh faced financial difficulties and periods of emotional distress. His brother Theo, an art dealer, supported him financially and emotionally and remained one of his closest sources of support. Their relationship is documented in many letters that reveal Vincent's thoughts about art, life, and his work. Vincent van Gogh died on July 29, 1890, in Auvers-sur-Oise, France, at the age of 37. Although he achieved little commercial success during his lifetime, his work became widely appreciated after his death. Today, he is remembered for his bold use of color, expressive brushstrokes, and ability to communicate emotion through painting. His art continues to inspire artists and people around the world.

  • 1880: Decided to dedicate himself to painting.
  • 1886: Moved to Paris and discovered Impressionism.
  • 1888: Moved to Arles and painted Sunflowers and The Bedroom.
  • 1889: Painted The Starry Night.

His works

1. The Starry Night (1889)

The Starry Night is one of Van Gogh's most famous paintings. It shows a small village beneath a bright night sky filled with swirling clouds, shining stars, and a glowing moon. The deep blue tones contrast with the yellow lights in the sky, creating a dreamlike atmosphere. The movement of the brushstrokes makes the sky appear alive and expresses the artist's imagination and emotions.

2. The Bedroom (1888)

In The Bedroom, Van Gogh painted his own bedroom in the Yellow House in Arles. The painting shows a bed, chairs, paintings on the walls, and other simple pieces of furniture. He used strong colors, including blue, yellow, and green, to create contrast and give the room a distinctive appearance. Through this everyday scene, he wanted to communicate a feeling of rest and comfort while showing how color could influence the mood of a space.

3. Irises (1890)

Irises is a painting of flowers that Van Gogh created while staying at an asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence. The composition features blue and purple irises surrounded by green leaves, with a contrasting background that makes the flowers stand out. The curved lines and energetic brushstrokes give the plants a sense of movement. This work shows his close attention to nature and his ability to use color and texture to make a simple subject visually interesting.

4. The Potato Eaters (1885)

The Potato Eaters is one of Van Gogh's most important early paintings. It depicts a peasant family gathered around a table, sharing a meal of potatoes under the light of a small lamp. Unlike his later paintings, this work uses dark, earthy colors such as brown, green, and gray. Van Gogh wanted to show the difficult lives of working people and represent them honestly. The rough features and simple surroundings emphasize the connection between the family and their daily labor.

My favorites paintings

These are my three favorite paintings by Vincent van Gogh. Each one catches my attention in a different way. I love how he uses colors and brushstrokes to make simple things, like flowers, branches, or a quiet night, feel special. For me, his paintings are not just beautiful to look at; they also make me feel something.

1. Vase with Irises (1890)

I love this painting because of the flowers and the way their colors stand out against the background. The different shades of blue and purple make the irises look delicate, but the strong brushstrokes give them so much character. I also like that every flower looks a little different. To me, the painting shows how something as simple as a vase of flowers can become beautiful when you take the time to really look at it.

2. Almond Blossom (1890)

This painting is one of my favorites because it gives me a feeling of peace and happiness. I love the contrast between the soft blue background and the delicate white blossoms. The branches seem to reach across the canvas, making the whole painting feel light and full of life. I also like knowing that almond trees bloom at the beginning of spring because, to me, this painting represents new beginnings, hope, and the beauty of starting over.

3. Starry Night Over the Rhône (1888)

This painting is another favorite of mine because of how the stars and gas lights reflect on the water. It gives a peaceful atmosphere that makes the night feel calm and magical.
